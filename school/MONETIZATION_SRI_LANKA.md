# Punchi Nena Maga monetization — Sri Lanka (checked 2026-11-22)

Primary website: https://punchinanamaga.blogspot.com/

## Priority 1 — Adsterra
Publisher registration: https://publishers.adsterra.com/
- Adsterra officially lists Sri Lanka (LKR) among local-currency Hyperwallet bank transfer destinations.
- Official April 2026 payout article: local bank transfer threshold USD 25, USD 7 fee plus currency conversion fee; ID/KYC required. Final methods and fees are account/country dependent.
- Source: https://adsterra.com/blog/payouts-in-local-currency/
- Add Blogger domain to Websites and request an educational-content-appropriate native or banner placement. For Blogger, use Layout > Add a Gadget > HTML/JavaScript after obtaining the **real** approved ad snippet. Do not invent a code or ad URL.
- Source: https://adsterra.com/blog/how-put-ads-on-your-website-and-get-paid/
- Keep visitors on Blogger. Clearly label sponsored units; no forced advertiser redirects, interstitial lock screens, fake download buttons or required ad clicks.

## Priority 2 — Google AdSense
Registration: https://www.google.com/adsense/start/
- Google's current country table lists Sri Lanka with Check and Wire transfers for AdSense (not EFT).
- Source: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1714397
- Need approval and site-policy compliance. Do not place AdSense code until the correct publisher ID is issued. Take special care with school-age audiences.

## Conditional option — Monetag
https://monetag.com/
- Country-dependent payouts (e.g. Payoneer $20/$30, wire $500 with possible fees); official product page says free-hosted blogspot.com is not supported. Verify current eligibility with support before starting.
- Sources: https://help.monetag.com/en/articles/6745951-payment-methods-available-in-monetag and https://monetag.com/

## Revenue and quality checks
- No network approval, account, script, payment, or revenue is created by this repository document.
- Never disclose bank numbers, login passwords or payout tokens in GitHub, chat, or Facebook.
- Track organic visitors, Facebook referral clicks, ad impressions, verified revenue, payout minimum and actual payout confirmation separately.
- Never click your own ads, buy fake traffic, or promise returns.
- Keep Facebook post links clearly destined for the Blogger blog or an explicitly labeled advertising page.
- Do not layer competing ad networks in the same placements without confirming each provider's terms.

## Next authorized setup steps
1. Owner registers as Adsterra Publisher using correct residence country and completes email verification.
2. Owner reviews payouts available for Sri Lanka, submits required identity and bank information **inside Adsterra only**.
3. Owner adds Blogspot site and gets approval and a genuine banner/native ad code.
4. Insert that code in the owned Blogger template (requires Blogger editing access), check mobile display and direct access to lessons.
5. Apply for Google AdSense once core original articles, policy pages, and site navigation are ready.
